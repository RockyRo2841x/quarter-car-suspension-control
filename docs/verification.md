## Independent equation-level comparison

`analysis/compare.py` was added during preparation. It reads plant constants and supported PID settings from the original SLX archives and integrates the corresponding ordinary differential equations using SciPy. The original model files are not modified.

Controller reproduction uses `e=-x`, integral state `q'=e`, filter state `f'=N*(e-f)` and force `Fa=Kp*e+Ki*q+Kd*N*(e-f)`. The passive plant has `Fa=0`. The two road signals are explicitly defined in the script:

- 0 to 0.05 m step at 1 second.
- 0.03 m sine wave at 1 rad/s.

All states start at zero. Each run covers 0–10 seconds with output sampled every 0.002 seconds. Adaptive integration uses `rtol=1e-8`, `atol=1e-10`, with maximum step 0.005 seconds. Each active/passive scenario was repeated at a 0.0025-second maximum step.

Passed checks:

- Original active/passive vehicle parameters match.
- PID configuration is continuous, parallel, derivative-filtered and unsaturated.
- The zero-input, zero-state equilibrium has zero derivatives.
- With actuator gains zero, the active plant's four mechanical derivatives match the passive plant.
- All integrations complete successfully with finite outputs.
- Maximum body/wheel displacement difference after maximum-step refinement is approximately `2.66e-10 m`, below the required `1e-6 m` threshold.

These checks support this numerical reproduction. They do not demonstrate equivalence of every Simulink block setting or validate a real suspension.

## Results and interpretation

The JSON metrics and two CSV files were generated by the comparison script. RMS values use uniformly sampled full-window data, including transients; acceleration is evaluated from the plant equations at those samples. Reported peaks are sampled peaks rather than a continuous-time peak search.

For the 50 mm step, active body-displacement RMS is lower, but acceleration RMS rises from about 0.7269 to 0.7769 m/s². Maximum absolute suspension travel rises from about 66.63 to 73.74 mm, with peak actuator force about 2529.4 N. For the sine case, active acceleration RMS falls from about 0.03851 to 0.01066 m/s², with peak force about 438.0 N.

The displacement metric includes the step's static offset from the zero reference. Force capacity, power consumption, stroke limits and frequency-weighted passenger comfort are not evaluated. The original actuator has no saturation, so the results cannot establish a feasible hardware design.

The generated plot was visually inspected for legible axes, units, legends and explicit independent-analysis labeling. The two model previews were extracted from the originals' embedded thumbnails and visually inspected; they are historical diagrams, not new screenshots of running Simulink models.
